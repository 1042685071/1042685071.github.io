Why choose SPC flooring

Market-Ready SPC Flooring for Residential and Commercial Projects

SPC flooring is widely used in apartments, hotels, offices, retail stores and renovation projects where waterproof performance, dimensional stability, easy maintenance and fast installation are important.

Waterproof by Design

The rigid SPC core helps resist swelling, making it a practical choice for kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms, and basements.

Enhanced Fire Resistance

A mineral-based rigid core offers improved heat resistance compared with many wood-based flooring products.

Low-VOC Material Profile

Built with a low-VOC material profile and no added formaldehyde for indoor flooring applications.

Fast Click-Lock Installation

A glue-free locking system keeps planks tight and even, helping reduce installation time and improve edge stability.
